How to Request Data Deletion

If you want to delete your Purna City account and request deletion of your associated personal data, follow these simple steps.

  1. Select whether you are a Customer or a Seller.
  2. Your email application will open with a prepared deletion request.
  3. Check the email and send it to our support address.
  4. Our team may contact you to verify your account ownership.
  5. We will review your request and process it according to applicable legal requirements.

Send your request to: deepaksoftware98@gmail.com

Important Information

Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate accounting, security, or dispute-resolution purposes. We will review your request and explain any applicable limitations.
