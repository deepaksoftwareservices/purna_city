Your Privacy Matters

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Purna City collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our food and grocery delivery application, website, and related services.

Effective Date: 16 October 2026
Last Updated: 16 October 2026

1Introduction

Welcome to Purna City ("we", "our", "us", or "Purna City"). Purna City provides an online platform that enables customers to order food and grocery products from participating restaurants and grocery sellers and receive deliveries through delivery partners.

This policy describes our information-handling practices. Where required by law, we will obtain consent before collecting or processing personal information.

2Information We Collect

Customer Information

  • Name and contact number.
  • Email address, if provided.
  • Account and authentication identifiers.
  • Delivery address and location details.
  • Food and grocery orders and order history.
  • Customer support messages and feedback.
  • Payment status and transaction references.

Location Information

If you grant location permission, we may use your device's location to find nearby stores, determine delivery destinations, estimate distances, and support delivery services.

Order Information

  • Items ordered, quantities, prices, and applicable charges.
  • Restaurant or grocery seller details.
  • Order status and delivery instructions.
  • Delivery verification information, such as a PIN where used.

Seller Information

Sellers may provide their name, business name, contact details, store address, location, product information, verification details, and settlement information where required.

Delivery Partner Information

Delivery partners may provide their name, phone number, account details, vehicle or licence information where required, delivery location updates, assigned orders, and earnings records.

Technical Information

Depending on the services enabled, we may collect app version, device and operating system details, crash reports, and diagnostic information.

3How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage customer, seller, and delivery partner accounts.
  • Display nearby restaurants and grocery stores.
  • Process and fulfill food and grocery orders.
  • Coordinate food preparation, pickup, and delivery.
  • Calculate delivery distances and applicable charges.
  • Send order confirmations and delivery updates.
  • Verify deliveries and help prevent fraud or misuse.
  • Manage seller settlements and delivery partner payments.
  • Respond to support requests and complaints.
  • Maintain security, troubleshoot errors, and improve services.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4Location Permissions

Customer location may be used to find nearby stores and identify the delivery destination. Delivery partner location may be used to coordinate assigned deliveries and provide delivery updates.

Location access depends on the permissions you grant and the features implemented in the app. If background location is used, it should be disclosed clearly and handled in accordance with applicable permission requirements.

You can manage location permissions in your device settings. Certain location-dependent features may not work if permission is denied.

5How We Share Information

Restaurants and Grocery Sellers

Sellers may receive order details and the customer information reasonably necessary to prepare and fulfill an order.

Delivery Partners

Assigned delivery partners may receive the delivery address, necessary contact details, order information, and instructions required to complete a delivery.

Technology Providers

Depending on our configuration, we may use providers for hosting, authentication, databases, notifications, maps, payment processing, analytics, and crash reporting.

Firebase services may support authentication, database, hosting, and other app functions.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information where required by applicable law or valid legal process, or where necessary to protect the safety, rights, and security of users or our platform.

We do not sell personal information to third parties in exchange for money.

6Payments

Payments may be handled by the payment methods and providers made available in the app. We may receive transaction identifiers, amounts, and payment status.

External payment providers may process information under their own privacy policies. Our handling of payment information depends on the actual payment integration we use.

7Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

These measures may include access controls, authentication, and restricted database permissions. No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

8Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes described in this policy.

Certain order, accounting, security, dispute, or legally required records may need to be retained for applicable periods. When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete or anonymize it where reasonably practicable and lawful.

9Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent and manage location or notification permissions through your device settings.

Some information may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes. Contact us to make a privacy request; we may need to verify your identity.

10Account Deletion

You may request account deletion through an available in-app feature or by contacting our support team. We will process eligible requests in accordance with applicable law.

Some transaction or legally required records may be retained where permitted or required by law. Uninstalling the app does not itself delete your account or server-stored data.

11Children's Privacy

Purna City does not knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental consent is required without the necessary consent.

If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

12Third-Party Services

Our app or website may link to third-party websites, maps, payment providers, or other services. Those providers may have their own privacy policies and practices.

We encourage you to review their policies when using their services.

13Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for necessary functionality, security, preferences, or analytics, depending on its configuration. Where required by law, we will provide appropriate information and choices.

14Data Processing and Transfers

Our service providers may process or store information in different regions or countries, depending on their infrastructure. Where applicable, we will take steps to meet relevant legal requirements for such processing.

15Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services, technology, or legal obligations change. We will publish the revised policy with an updated date and provide notice or obtain consent where required by law.

16Applicable Law

This policy is intended for a service operating in India and should be applied consistently with applicable Indian data protection, information technology, and other relevant legal requirements.
